## Product Hunt description

Peeky is a calm Windows companion for healthier screen time. It schedules blink, look-away, posture, and walking breaks using active computer time, then intelligently bundles nearby reminders so breaks never pile up. It lives quietly in the system tray, works offline, and includes an optional local activity journal. No account, cloud sync, telemetry, screenshots, keystrokes, or browser history.

## Maker comment

Hey Product Hunt,

I built Peeky because most break reminders eventually became another thing I wanted to dismiss.

The problem was not the idea of taking breaks. It was the interruption design. A blink reminder would appear, then a posture reminder, then another prompt just after I had already stepped away. The tool was technically working, but the experience was not calm.

Peeky approaches that differently. It tracks active computer time, pauses while you are away, and follows a simple hierarchy:

Blink -> Look Away -> Correct Posture -> Walk Away

When a larger break is close, Peeky bundles the smaller actions into it. Finishing a Walk Away break also covers posture, looking away, and blinking, so you do not return to a queue of reminders.

It runs quietly in the Windows system tray, works offline, and keeps settings and optional activity history on your computer. There are no accounts, ads, telemetry, screenshots, keystrokes, cloud classification, or browser-tab tracking.

Peeky 2.0 is the version where the scheduling finally feels like the product I wanted to use every day. I would genuinely value feedback on the break rhythm, the interruption experience, and what feels helpful versus distracting.

Thanks for taking a look.

## Key points for the launch page

- Four calm break types: Blink, Look Away, Correct Posture, and Walk Away.
- Break Flood Protection bundles nearby lower-priority reminders into larger breaks.
- Timers follow active computer use and pause while you are idle, locked, sleeping, or outside active hours.
- A five-second heads-up lets you start immediately or snooze.
- System tray controls keep Peeky available without occupying the taskbar.
- Optional local activity sessions show where active computer time went.
- Everything works locally with no account or telemetry.
- Available for Windows 10 and 11 as an installer or portable ZIP.

## Community reply snippets

**Why Windows only?**

Peeky relies on Windows-native tray, foreground, idle, lock, and installer behavior. I wanted one platform to feel complete before expanding elsewhere.

**How is this different from a timer?**

Peeky measures active computer use, pauses when you step away, understands break priority, and bundles nearby reminders so the schedule does not flood you.

**What data does it collect?**

Break settings stay local. Optional activity tracking records foreground application names and active durations locally. It does not capture screenshots, keystrokes, clipboard contents, files, browser tabs, URLs, or page content.

**Is it free?**

Yes. Peeky 2.0 is free to download for Windows 10 and 11.

**Does it need an account or internet connection?**

No. Peeky works offline and does not require an account.
